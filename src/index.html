Good to know

Quick answers for newcomers.

Are events free?

Most campus events are free. Some workshops or trips may require a small fee to cover materials.

Do I need to register?

Registration depends on the event capacity. Check the event listing for details.

Can I bring friends?

Often yes—unless the event is limited to members or requires a campus ID.

How can I stay updated?

Bookmark this page and check the featured section weekly to see what's trending.
